1. Return Eligibility

We offer a 7-day return window from the date of delivery for most products, provided:

  • The product is unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging with tags intact.
  • You have proof of purchase (order confirmation via WhatsApp).
  • The product is not part of our non-returnable category (see Section 2).

2. Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, the following items cannot be returned unless defective or damaged on arrival: personal care and beauty products that have been opened or used, and innerwear.

3. How to Request a Return

  1. Message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery with your order details.
  2. Share clear photos/videos of the product and issue (if damaged/defective).
  3. Our team will review and confirm return pickup or drop-off instructions.

4. Refund Process

Once the returned product is received and inspected, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method (UPI) or as store credit, based on your preference.

5. Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a damaged, defective, or wrong item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photo/video proof. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund.

6. Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct exchanges. If you'd like a different size, color, or product, please initiate a return and place a new order.

7. Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped by messaging us on WhatsApp. Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled but may be returned as per this policy after delivery.

8. Contact

For any return or refund queries, please reach out via our Contact Us page or WhatsApp.
