About YantraPlus

YantraPlus is a premium Indian spiritual and devotional products brand created to bring the essence of traditional Indian worship into modern homes.

From incense sticks and dhoop to hawan cups, pooja samagri and Rudraksha products, every item in our collection is thoughtfully created to complement your daily rituals and sacred moments.

Our Philosophy

Tradition in every ritual. Devotion in every product. A divine touch in every home.

We believe spirituality belongs in everyday life — not just on special occasions. Our products are created to make daily worship simple, meaningful and beautiful.

Our Unique Story — Inspired by Kashi

Kashi represents one of India's deepest spiritual traditions. YantraPlus takes inspiration from this sacred heritage to create products that bring a sense of devotion into everyday life.

We believe spirituality does not have to be complicated. Sometimes, it begins with a small ritual — lighting an incense stick, preparing the home mandir, performing a pooja or sitting quietly for a few moments of meditation.

YantraPlus is created for these moments.

What Makes YantraPlus Different

  • Premium spiritual products — created for customers who value quality and devotional experience.
  • Kashi-inspired heritage — a brand identity connected with the sacred spiritual traditions of Kashi.
  • Thoughtful presentation — premium visual identity and packaging designed to build trust.
  • Everyday usability — products suitable for regular pooja and spiritual routines.
  • Wide devotional range — incense, dhoop, hawan products, pooja essentials and spiritual accessories under one brand.
