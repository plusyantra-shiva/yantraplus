1. Introduction

At ShopEase ("we", "us", "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as name, email address and phone number, when you reach out via our Contact form or WhatsApp.
  • Order-related information, including the products you purchase and payment confirmation screenshots sent for order verification.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and device information, used only to improve site performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your orders.
  • To communicate with you about order status, delivery, and support queries.
  • To improve our website, products, and customer experience.
  • To respond to inquiries submitted through our Contact form or WhatsApp.

4. Payment Information

We do not store your UPI PIN, bank account details, or card information at any point. All UPI payments are processed directly through your chosen UPI app. We only receive a payment confirmation screenshot that you voluntarily share with us via WhatsApp for order verification purposes.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your information may only be shared with delivery partners strictly for the purpose of fulfilling your order.

6. Cookies

This website may use basic browser storage to remember preferences like your last search or filter selection. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by us at any time by contacting us via the details on our Contact Us page.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Us page.
