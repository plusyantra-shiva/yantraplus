Home / Shipping Policy

1. Shipping Coverage

We currently ship to most serviceable pin codes across India. If you're unsure whether we deliver to your area, message us on WhatsApp before placing an order.

2. Order Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days after payment verification. You will receive a WhatsApp update once your order has been dispatched.

3. Delivery Timelines

  • Metro cities: 3-5 business days
  • Other cities & towns: 5-7 business days
  • Remote areas: 7-10 business days

These are estimated timelines and may vary due to courier delays, weather, or other unforeseen circumstances.

4. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges (if any) are calculated based on product weight, dimensions, and delivery location, and will be clearly shown before you complete your payment. We may offer free shipping on select products or order values from time to time.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, we will share tracking details via WhatsApp so you can monitor your delivery status.

6. Delayed or Missing Orders

If your order hasn't arrived within the expected timeframe, please contact us via WhatsApp or our Contact Us page with your order details, and we'll investigate promptly.

7. Failed Delivery Attempts

Please ensure someone is available at the delivery address and that contact details provided are accurate. Repeated failed delivery attempts due to incorrect address/unavailability may result in the order being returned to us, and re-shipping charges may apply.

8. Contact

For any shipping-related questions, please reach out via our Contact Us page.
